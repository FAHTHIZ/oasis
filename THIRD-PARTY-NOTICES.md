Licenses that need attention:

- `dompurify` (MPL-2.0 OR Apache-2.0): used under Apache-2.0.
- `@zone-eu/mailsplit` (MIT OR EUPL-1.1+), pulled in by `imapflow` and
  `mailparser` for the email gateway: used under MIT.
- `nodemailer` (MIT-0): MIT without the attribution requirement.
- `node-forge` (BSD-3-Clause OR GPL-2.0), pulled in by
  `@anthropic-ai/sandbox-runtime`: used under BSD-3-Clause.
- `elkjs` (EPL-2.0), pulled in by `beautiful-mermaid` for diagram layout. It
  is used unmodified as a separate module. EPL-2.0 requires that its source
  stays available, which it is at <https://github.com/kieler/elkjs>, and that
  modifications to it are published under EPL-2.0. OASIS does not modify it.
- `sharp` prebuilt binaries (`@img/sharp-*`, `@img/sharp-libvips-*`),
  LGPL-3.0-or-later for libvips. `sharp` is an optional dependency for image
  resizing; libvips is dynamically linked and can be replaced by the user, as
  the LGPL requires. OASIS works without it.

No dependency is under the GPL, AGPL, SSPL or a source-available license
without an open-source alternative.

## Desktop app

The desktop installers bundle [Electron](https://github.com/electron/electron)
(MIT), which includes Chromium and its dependencies. Electron ships their
notices in `LICENSES.chromium.html` next to the app binary. The desktop app's
own npm dependencies are listed in `desktop/package-lock.json`.
