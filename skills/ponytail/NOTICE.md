# Ponytail (bundled)

The ponytail, ponytail-review, ponytail-audit, ponytail-debt, ponytail-gain and
ponytail-help skills are bundled from [DietrichGebert/ponytail](https://github.com/DietrichGebert/ponytail)
v5.3.2 (commit 09c434ab73ca43761c5d8b0c5d341926c2fba710), MIT License, Copyright (c) Dietrich Gebert.
See LICENSE in this folder. Content is unchanged.

Use it in Oasis with `/ponytail` (levels: lite, full, ultra, off), `/ponytail-review`,
`/ponytail-audit`, `/ponytail-debt`, `/ponytail-gain` and `/ponytail-help`.
