# OASIS brand

**Primary tagline:** Code. Automate. Flourish.

OASIS is an open-source AI coding agent and local-first developer workspace. Its mark is the selected lime arch with an inner contour and horizontal strata that taper inward and down. Keep the silhouette consistent: no generic ring, orb, camera lens, or “OSS” lockup.

## Hero direction

The README hero is a panoramic 1920 × 640 composition: portal mark on the left, OASIS wordmark on the right, and a restrained green glow over a reflective grid floor. Use the product name as **OASIS**, without an “OSS” suffix. The words “open-source” may appear in descriptive copy, but should not be attached to the wordmark.

Keep the hero legible at reduced widths: one primary tagline, one short product description, and a compact capability line. Avoid repeating the headline immediately below the banner or packing the image with tiny feature paragraphs.
